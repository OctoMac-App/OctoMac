Every printer at a glance

Live from OctoPrint

All printers, side by side

Add as many OctoPrint servers as you like. Each one shows its status, temperatures and progress in the sidebar, and All printers puts every camera and print on one screen.

OctoMac listens to OctoPrint's live connection, so temperatures, progress and events appear the moment they happen. It finds OctoPrint on your network for you, and copes with self-signed HTTPS and printers behind a reverse proxy.

Always within reach

Even with OctoMac closed

Desktop and Notification Centre widgets, a Control Centre control, a menu bar helper and a progress bar on the Dock icon. Notifications for finished, failed and paused prints arrive with a picture from the camera.

Full control

Move, heat, console

Heat up with your saved presets, move the print head, extrude, adjust fan, speed and flow, and send G-code from a console that hides the chatter you don't need, with a cheat sheet beside it.

Watch it print

Without hovering over the printer

A 3D preview that follows along

See exactly what will be printed, layer by layer or as a model you can spin around. While printing, a little nozzle glides along the toolpath at the printer's own pace, and the view moves up with it layer by layer.

The camera has a cinema mode, one-click snapshots and a picture in picture window that floats over your other apps. Record a GIF or MP4 of the last ten seconds, the next few, or a quick timelapse, ready to share.

Layer alerts

Magnets, nuts, colour changes

Pause at a layer to drop something in, or just get a notification when the print gets there. Once the first layer is down, OctoMac sends a picture of it, with Pause and Stop Print buttons, so a bad start never runs for hours.

When the printer needs you

MMU, failed parts, stops

With a Prusa MMU, OctoMac asks which filament to load when the printer does. If one part fails, stop just that part while the rest carry on. Stopping the whole print always asks first, and there's a separate emergency stop for when something goes wrong.

Files and history

Print in a couple of clicks

Your files, as thumbnails

Drag and drop

Browse files with their slicer thumbnails, check the print time and filament, then press Print. Drag G-code in to upload it, or open it from Finder. Send a file to another printer, or to several at once.

Plan it: schedule a print to be done by 8am, or queue up the next few. When one finishes, OctoMac asks you to clear the bed and starts the next.

Print times come from Print Time Genius when it's installed, so they're usually close.

History and statistics

One printer or all of them

See what finished and what didn't, the hours and filament it all took, and a month-by-month chart. Rate prints and note what worked, so it's there next time. Play, save or delete OctoPrint's timelapse videos right there.

Look back

The History tab keeps the numbers that matter: how many prints finished, your success rate, printing time and filament used, and which files you print most.

Know your machine

Before it lets you down

The Machine tab

Your printer's firmware, the Raspberry Pi's power and temperature health, and a 3D bed level map you can measure from OctoMac. Compare today's bed with an earlier measurement to see exactly what's moved.

Install OctoPrint and plugin updates with one click, add plugins from OctoPrint's official list, switch them on or off, and read or save OctoPrint's logs. If a printer won't connect, OctoMac tells you why in plain words.

Get OctoMac

Free, for Apple silicon and Intel Macs

1Download

Open the disk image and drag OctoMac into Applications. It's signed and notarised by Apple, and keeps itself up to date.

Download for Mac

Or use Homebrew

$ brew tap octomac-app/tap
$ brew trust octomac-app/tap
$ brew install --cask octomac

Homebrew 7 and later ask you to trust taps outside their own.

2Add a printer

Pick your printer from those OctoMac finds on your network, choose Request access and approve it in OctoPrint. OctoMac needs an OctoPrint admin account and macOS 15 Sequoia or newer.

No printer handy? Choose Help → Try Demo Mode.

Private by design

OctoMac talks straight to your printers. There are no accounts, no tracking and no servers of ours in between. API keys stay on your Mac, in the Keychain. Read the privacy note.

Free and open source

Found a problem? Choose Help → Report a Problem… in OctoMac and open an issue on GitHub. If OctoMac makes your printing easier, you can buy me a coffee.
